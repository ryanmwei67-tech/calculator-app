Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. CALCX is designed to keep data collection minimal and transparent.

What we collect

CALCX is built to be simple and privacy-friendly. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the calculator.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or other personal data when you use the app.

How it works

Most calculator activity happens locally on your device. Expressions and results are processed in your browser and are not normally transmitted to our servers.

Any information you voluntarily share with us, such as through email or a support request, will only be used for the purpose you provided it.

Cookies & ads

We may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, which may use cookies and similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure ad performance.

These services operate under their own privacy policies, and you can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the site. However, no digital system can be guaranteed completely secure, so we encourage safe browsing habits and careful handling of any personal information you choose to share.

Third-party services

Because CALCX may use advertising or analytics tools, third-party providers may process technical data such as device information, browser type, and interactions with the site in accordance with their own policies. We do not sell personal data.

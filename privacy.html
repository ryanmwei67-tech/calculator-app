Privacy Policy

CALCX respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our calculator app handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

CALCX is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not require you to create an account, provide your name, or submit personal information to use the calculator.

We do not intentionally collect, store, or sell your personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or financial details.

2. Local Use of the App

Calculations are processed locally in your browser. Any numbers or expressions you enter are generally not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly share them with us, such as by emailing us or submitting a support request.

3. Cookies and Advertising

Our website may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to personalize ads, measure ad performance, and improve user experience.

Cookies may also be used to remember preferences such as theme settings, if supported by the browser or site configuration.

4. Third-Party Services

Because we may use advertising partners and analytics tools, those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external service you interact with.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we aim to keep data handling minimal and transparent.

6. Children's Privacy

CALCX is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

You can limit or disable cookies in your browser settings. You can also avoid interacting with advertising networks or third-party trackers if you prefer not to receive personalized ads.

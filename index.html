    About CALCX

    CALCX is a free online calculator designed to help students, professionals, and everyday users perform quick and accurate calculations. It is fast, simple, and works on all devices including mobile phones.

    How to Use

    Enter numbers using the buttons, choose an operation (+, −, ×, ÷), and press "=" to get your result. You can also view your previous calculations in the history section.

    Features

    • Fast calculations
    • History tracking
    • Mobile-friendly design
    • Simple and clean interface
